3) APP FUNCTIONS, AUDIENCE, PROBLEM / VALUE
Abroadster is a social app for university students studying abroad.
Problem: students arrive abroad without an easy way to see classmates nearby, plan weekend trips, share albums, or stay in one program community.
Value: a live social map of classmates and trips, plus feed, chat, trip albums, and program-scoped community features so students can meet people and coordinate travel in one place.
Target audience: college / university students (13+) who are studying abroad or preparing to.
