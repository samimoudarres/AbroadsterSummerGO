Abroadster — what YOU still do for App Store (no MacBook required for most of this)

============================================================
A) ONE interactive terminal command (Apple login) — REQUIRED
============================================================
In Cursor terminal, project folder:

  $env:EXPO_TOKEN="(your expo token from .env)"
  $env:EAS_NO_VCS="1"
  npx eas-cli@latest build --platform ios --profile production

When asked:
  1. Log in with your Apple Developer Apple ID
  2. Select your Team
  3. Let Expo generate Distribution Certificate + Provisioning Profile (YES)
  4. Wait for the cloud iOS build (~15–25 min)

You do NOT need your Mac for this — EAS builds on Expo’s Macs.

After it finishes, open the build page and confirm status = Finished.


============================================================
B) App Store Connect (browser on any PC) — REQUIRED
============================================================
1. Go to https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. My Apps → + → New App
   - Platforms: iOS
   - Name: Abroadster
   - Bundle ID: com.abroadster.app  (create it in Certificates/Identifiers if missing)
   - SKU: abroadster-ios-001
3. Paste listing text from: store/APP_STORE_LISTING.txt
4. Privacy Policy URL (paste exactly):
   https://ajwnvwvpvasxkwpfdsvo.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/legal/privacy.html
5. Upload screenshots (see section D)
6. Upload 1024×1024 marketing icon (same as assets/app-icon-1024.png)
7. Age rating questionnaire → 13+
8. App Privacy answers → see store/APP_STORE_LISTING.txt


============================================================
C) Submit the IPA from Windows — NO MAC
============================================================
After the iOS build finishes:

  $env:EXPO_TOKEN="..."
  $env:EAS_NO_VCS="1"
  npx eas-cli@latest submit --platform ios --latest --profile production

Follow prompts to pick the App Store Connect app.
Then in ASC: select the build → Add for Review → Submit.


============================================================
D) Screenshots (can do without Mac)
============================================================
Options:
  - Record from your Android preview build / Expo web and crop to iPhone sizes
  - Use a design tool to frame UI into iPhone 6.7" and 6.1" templates
  - Later on MacBook: Simulator screenshots (optional)

Required typical sizes ASC will ask for:
  - 6.7" (iPhone 15 Pro Max class)
  - Often also 6.1"


============================================================
E) Only if you want Xcode later (OPTIONAL)
============================================================
MacBook is optional for:
  - Local debugging with Instruments
  - Capturing Simulator screenshots
NOT required for build or submit when using EAS.
