## Description
Abroadster is the social map for students studying abroad.

Find classmates and friends nearby, plan trips together, share albums from every city, and stay in the loop with DMs and school community chats.

• See friends on the map by live city (when they allow location) or study-abroad city
• Create and lock in group trips
• Follow trip albums and stamp posts
• Message friends and join school communities
• Build your passport as you explore

Abroadster is made for university students living and traveling abroad.

## Review Notes (paste into App Review Information → Notes)
Abroadster is a social app for university students studying abroad (13+).

AGE ASSURANCE (Guideline 2.3.6) — READ FIRST
Parental Controls: NO. Age Assurance: YES.
Demo login skips sign-up, so age assurance is NOT shown when using demo credentials.
To verify: Sign out → Create account → Name → Email → Password → "Verify your age" → enter DOB under 13 → blocked.
Also: Profile → Settings → Age requirement (13+).

HOW TO SIGN IN (main features)
Demo: samimoudarres@gmail.com / testaccount
Or Sign up with Email and complete onboarding (birthday must be 13+).

DEMO CONTENT
Production builds use live Supabase data (demo seed is off). After signup you will see the map with study-abroad city pins, trips, feed, and chats based on real backend data. If the map looks empty at first, pan/zoom around popular study cities (e.g. Paris) or search a city in the map search bar.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile → Settings → Delete account
Also: https://abroadster.vercel.app/delete-account

MODERATION
Users can Report and Block other users from profile menus. Reports go to our moderation flow / samimoudarres@hotmail.com.

PERMISSIONS
• Photos / Camera — only when the user chooses to upload a profile photo, post, album photo, or chat attachment
• Notifications — optional; requested after signup so users can get DMs / trip updates
• Location — When-In-Use only, while using the map, so friends see your actual city. Decline the prompt to stay on your host-city pin.

WHAT TO TEST
• Age assurance: sign out → Create account → Verify your age (under-13 blocked)
• Map tab: pan, search a city, open a person / trip pin
• Create or view a trip; open trip album
• Send a DM (use two accounts if possible)
• Settings → Delete account (use throwaway account)
