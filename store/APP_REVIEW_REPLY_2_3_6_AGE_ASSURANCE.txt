Thank you for reviewing Abroadster again. We understand the confusion and have clarified below.

Our Age Rating answers are:
• Parental Controls: NO
• Age Assurance: YES

Abroadster does not include parental controls. We do include age assurance: users must enter their date of birth during sign-up, and users under 13 cannot create an account or access any social features (map, feed, chat, trips, messaging).

Why the reviewer may not have seen it:
The demo login credentials we provided skip the sign-up flow entirely, so the age assurance step is not shown when signing in with the demo account.

How to locate age assurance (please follow exactly):
1. Launch Abroadster on the review device.
2. If signed in, go to Profile → Settings → Log out.
3. On the Welcome screen, note the text: "Abroadster is for ages 13+. Age is verified during sign-up."
4. Tap Create account (do not use Log in / demo credentials for this test).
5. Complete: Name → Email/phone → Password.
6. On the "Verify your age" screen, read the Age assurance card, then enter a date of birth that makes the user under 13 years old.
7. Tap Next. The app blocks account creation and shows: "You must be at least 13 years old to use Abroadster."
8. Enter a valid 13+ date of birth to continue onboarding.

Also in Settings (while signed in):
Profile → Settings → Support section → tap "Age requirement (13+)" for an explanation of the age assurance mechanism and how to view the sign-up verification step.

Our Privacy Policy and Terms also state the 13+ requirement:
https://abroadster.vercel.app/privacy
https://abroadster.vercel.app/terms

We have updated the in-app sign-up screens and Settings to make age assurance easier to find. Build [NEW BUILD NUMBER] includes these clarifications.

We respectfully ask you to re-review using the Create account flow above rather than the demo login when verifying age assurance.
